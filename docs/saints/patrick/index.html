St PatrickApostle of Ireland

St Patrick window, St Patrick's Church, Junction City, Ohio, photo by Nheyob · CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons

Lived5th century (exact dates uncertain ⚠)
Feast day17 March
RecognisedPre-Congregation

His story

Born in Roman Britain to a Christian family, Patrick was captured by Irish raiders at about 16 and spent six years herding animals in Ireland, where he turned to prayer. He escaped and went home, then came back as a bishop to evangelise the Irish. His own Confessio survives, one of the earliest documents of Irish history.

Path to sainthood

Venerated by popular acclaim long before the formal process existed (pre-Congregation).

Patronages

Ireland, Nigeria, engineers, and the Archdiocese of New York.

Trivia

  • He wasn't Irish: he was a Roman Briton.
  • There have been no snakes in Ireland since the last Ice Age, so the snake legend is symbolic.
  • The shamrock story (explaining the Trinity with a three-leaf clover) first appears in writing centuries after his death ⚠.
