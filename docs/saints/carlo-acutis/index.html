St Carlo AcutisTeenage computer whiz, the first millennial saint

Carlo Acutis memorial, Corpus Christi Church, Covent Garden (photo), AndyScott · CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons

Lived1991–2006, London–Monza
Feast day12 October
RecognisedCanonised 7 September 2025 (Leo XIV)

His story

Born in London to Italian parents and raised in Milan, Carlo Acutis loved video games, football and computers, and taught himself programming. He went to Mass daily from his First Communion, and built a website and exhibition cataloguing Eucharistic miracles around the world, which has since travelled to many countries. He died of acute leukaemia on 12 October 2006, aged 15, offering his suffering for the Pope and the Church. He is buried in Assisi, at the Sanctuary of the Spoliation.

Path to sainthood

Beatified in Assisi on 10 October 2020. His canonisation, first set for 27 April 2025, was postponed after Pope Francis died on 21 April 2025. Pope Leo XIV canonised him on 7 September 2025, together with Pier Giorgio Frassati.

Patronages

The internet (popularly proposed), young people.

Trivia

  • He called the Eucharist "my highway to Heaven".
  • He was born on 3 May 1991, making him the first saint of the millennial generation.
  • At his tomb in Assisi he is displayed in jeans, sweatshirt and trainers.
