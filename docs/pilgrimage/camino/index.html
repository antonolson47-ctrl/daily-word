Pilgrimage
Camino de SantiagoThe Way of St James to Santiago de Compostela

For more than a thousand years, pilgrims have walked across Europe to the tomb of the Apostle James the Greater in the Cathedral of Santiago de Compostela, in Galicia (northwest Spain). In 2025 a record 530,987 pilgrims received the Compostela certificate (Pilgrim Office statistics).

2027 is a Holy Year. The Holy Door opens on 31 December 2026 and stays open all of 2027. Expect the biggest crowds in modern history: book private beds early. Holy Year 2027 →

Quick answers

  • Shortest route that earns the Compostela: Camino Inglés from Ferrol (~112 km, 5 days), or Francés from Sarria (~114 km).
  • Most popular: Camino Francés (45.6% of 2025 pilgrims), then the two Portuguese routes (~36% combined).
  • Cheapest bed: Galician public albergues, €10 a night, no reservations.
