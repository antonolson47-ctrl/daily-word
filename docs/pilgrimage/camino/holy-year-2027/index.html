Holy Year 2027Xacobeo 2027: the Holy Door and what it means for your trip

A Compostelan Holy Year (Año Santo, or Xacobeo) happens whenever 25 July, the feast of St James, falls on a Sunday. The next one is 2027. After that come 2032, 2038 and 2049.

Date What happens
31 December 2026 The Holy Door (Puerta Santa, on Praza da Quintana) is solemnly opened
All of 2027 The door stays open; a plenary indulgence is offered under the usual conditions
25 July 2027 (Sunday) Feast of St James
31 December 2027 The Holy Door closes

The motto of the Holy Year is from Matthew 2:10: "they rejoiced exceedingly with great joy" on seeing the star (Archbishop's pastoral letter).

⚠ Confirm before you go - Opening time: reported moved to around midday on 31 Dec 2026, but the Cathedral's calendar still showed 5 pm when we checked. - Botafumeiro: the giant censer is out of service while scaffolding for the cimborrio (lantern tower) restoration covers its mechanism, with no return date announced as of Oct 2026. - Pilgrim masses: from 16 October 2026, the 9:30, 12:00 and 19:30 pilgrim masses move to the nearby church of San Agustín until further notice.

Check catedraldesantiago.es before your visit.
