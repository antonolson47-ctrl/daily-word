Pilgrimage › Camino de Santiago
When to goSeasons, crowds and weather
  • May–June and September–October are the most popular windows: mild weather, though they're also busy. In 2025, May and August were among the busiest months for arrivals in Santiago (santiago.es statistics).
  • July–August: hot on the Meseta and the Vía de la Plata, and the last 100 km are crowded with Spanish holidaymakers.
  • November–March: quiet and beautiful, but many albergues close, days are short and rain is constant in Galicia. The Napoleon route over the Pyrenees is closed in winter; use Valcarlos.
  • 2027 Holy Year: expect record numbers all year, peaking around 25 July (feast of St James).

⚠ Seasonal albergue closures change every year. Check Gronze for the places on your route.
