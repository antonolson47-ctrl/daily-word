Camino del NorteThe Northern Way along Spain's Cantabrian coast

Photo: The Bidasoa at Irún by Zarateman, CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons

Distance~820–832 km
Stages34–36 · 5–6 weeks
StartIrún
2025 pilgrims4.05%

Cliffs, beaches and green hills through the Basque Country, Cantabria and Asturias, passing San Sebastián, Bilbao and Santander, then turning inland in Galicia. Tougher than the Francés (constant ups and downs) and much quieter; its share has fallen nine years running.

Good to know

  • Fly into Bilbao, San Sebastián or Biarritz, then go to Irún.
  • At Villaviciosa you can switch to the Primitivo via Oviedo.
