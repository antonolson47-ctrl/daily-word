Albergues & daily costsWhere you sleep and what it costs in 2026

Types of albergue

Type Typical price Notes
Public (Xunta de Galicia network) €10 flat No reservations, one night only, open 13:00–22:00, out by 8:00, disposable sheets, credencial required
Municipal (outside Galicia) ~€8–15 ⚠ Set by each town
Parochial / donativo Donation Often with a shared meal; give generously
Private albergue ~€15–25 dorm Usually bookable; many have private rooms too
Pensión / hotel €40+ room Comfort, privacy, bookable

The Xunta's network has about 76 albergues with 3,000+ beds. You can't stay two nights for a rest day except for illness (Xunta de Galicia).

Daily budget (2026)

Style Per day
Lean: public albergues, supermarket, pilgrim menu ≈ €35
Typical: private albergues, café breakfast, pilgrim menu ≈ €50–60
Comfortable: private rooms, restaurant meals ≈ €100–140
Source: guiarutas.es 2026 cost guide. The Compostela itself is free.

Luggage transfer: Correos and private companies move your bag between stages for a fee.
