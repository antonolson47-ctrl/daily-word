Pilgrimage › Camino de Santiago
Camino InglésThe English Way, from the port of Ferrol
Distance~111–120 km
Stages5–6 days
StartFerrol
2025 pilgrims5.7%

Medieval pilgrims from England, Ireland and northern Europe sailed to Ferrol or A Coruña and walked from there. The shortest route that earns a full Compostela, and ideal for a single week.

⚠ Starting at A Coruña is only ~72 km, too short for the Compostela on its own. Start at Ferrol. (Pilgrims who started a route on foot outside Spain need at least 70 km in Spain; check the Pilgrim Office rules.)

Good to know

  • Fly into A Coruña or Santiago, then bus/train to Ferrol.
