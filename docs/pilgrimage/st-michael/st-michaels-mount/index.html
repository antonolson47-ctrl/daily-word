St Michael's MountMarazion, Cornwall, England
CountryEngland
TypeTidal island
AccessCauseway at low tide
Run byNational Trust & St Aubyn family

History. A tidal island crowned by a castle and church. In the Middle Ages it was a Benedictine priory linked to Mont-Saint-Michel. It later became a fortress, has been home to the St Aubyn family since the 17th century, and is cared for today by the National Trust together with the family.

Legend. Fishermen are said to have seen St Michael standing on the rock (traditionally in AD 495). Cornish folklore also says the giant Cormoran lived here until Jack the Giant Killer defeated him.

Visiting

  • The causeway is walkable only for a few hours around low tide. Use the Causeway Calculator. At high tide small boats run in season.
  • Autumn 2026 (13 Sep–30 Oct): castle open Sun–Fri 9:45–17:00 (last entry 15:45), closed Saturdays; garden closed (opening times).
  • The castle needs a ticket (prices). National Trust members get in free but must book.
