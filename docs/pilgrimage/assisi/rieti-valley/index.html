Spoleto and the Rieti ValleyThe southern way to Greccio, Fonte Colombo, Poggio Bustone and La Foresta

Sanctuaries4 around Rieti

South of Assisi the way follows the olive slopes of the Spoleto valley, climbs into the Valnerina, passes the lake of Piediluco and comes down into the Rieti valley, the Valle Santa (Holy Valley), ringed by four Franciscan sanctuaries Francis loved: Greccio, Fonte Colombo, Poggio Bustone and La Foresta.

Good to know

  • Stages: Assisi – Foligno 19.2 km; Foligno – Trevi 12.5 km; Trevi – Poreta 12 km; Poreta – Spoleto 15 km; Spoleto – Ceselli 15.7 km; Ceselli – Arrone 15 km; Arrone – Piediluco 12.8 km; Piediluco – Poggio Bustone 22 km. The Cammino di Francesco loop links the four Rieti sanctuaries.
  • Spoleto Cathedral keeps an autograph letter of Francis to Brother Leo.
  • Greccio has a live crib at Christmas and a museum of cribs.
  • The sanctuaries are friaries; check Mass and opening times locally.

Deep Dive

Greccio: the first crib

History. Thomas of Celano tells that for Christmas 1223 Francis asked his friend Giovanni Velita to prepare a manger with an ox and an ass in a cave at Greccio, "to see with bodily eyes" the poverty of the Child of Bethlehem. Mass was sung over the manger and Francis, a deacon, sang the Gospel and preached. Pope Francis wrote his letter on the crib, Admirabile signum, at Greccio in 2019.

Fonte Colombo and Poggio Bustone

History. At Fonte Colombo Francis wrote the final Rule in 1223, approved by Honorius III that November; here too he underwent cautery of his eyes. At Poggio Bustone, about 1208–09, he greeted the villagers with "Good morning, good people!" and, praying in a cave above, received assurance that his sins were forgiven; from here he sent the first brothers out two by two.

Legend. At La Foresta tradition says that Francis, sick and a guest of a poor priest, saw the man's vineyard trampled by visitors and promised a greater harvest; the vintage was more abundant than ever. Some also say he composed part of the Canticle of the Creatures here.
