About The Daily Word

The Daily Word started as an email to family and friends: one New Testament verse a day, read across three languages, with the setting it was spoken in. Every verse page shows:

  • Setting the scene: where this falls in Jesus' ministry (or, from Acts onward, who wrote it, where and for whom).
  • Four witnesses: the Greek (SBLGNT), the Latin Vulgate, the King James Version and the English Standard Version.
  • Key words, an alignment note on how closely the translations match, and a story from church history.

Translations and copyright

ESV verses quoted on this site so far: 65 (Crossway permits up to 500 verses on a website with its notice). Before reaching 500 we'll switch the modern English line to the NRSV (with permission) or a public-domain translation such as the Berean Standard Bible or World English Bible.
