On the "Bright Hill", **Jasna Góra**, above the city of Częstochowa, the **Pauline Fathers** have guarded since **1382** the icon of **Our Lady of Częstochowa**, the **Black Madonna**, with two scars on her cheek. It is the spiritual capital of Poland: through partitions, wars and Communism, Poles came here to pray for their nation, and still do in their hundreds of thousands, many on foot.

## Good to know
- **The icon** is shown in the **Chapel of the Miraculous Image**. It is **unveiled at 6:00 every day** to a fanfare, covered at **12:00** on weekdays (13:30 on Sundays and solemnities) and unveiled again at **13:30** on weekdays except Saturday (14:00 on Sundays). The **Jasna Góra Appeal** is sung before it every evening at **21:00**.
- **Opening:** the sanctuary gates are open about **5:00–21:45**; the monastery entrance (through the Knights' Hall) **5:30–21:00**. Mass in the chapel runs from 6:00 to 18:30 (20:00 on Sundays).
- **Also visit:** the **basilica**, the treasury and museums, the **bastions** with the outdoor Way of the Cross, and the 106 m **tower**.
- **August:** the great **walking pilgrimages** arrive, above all around **15 August** (Assumption) and **26 August** (the feast of Our Lady of Częstochowa). The Mass schedule changes; check the daily plan, published a day ahead.
- **Getting there:** trains from Kraków (about 1½–2 hours) and Warsaw (about 2½ hours) to Częstochowa; the shrine is a 20-minute walk up the avenue from the station.

## Deep Dive

### The icon
<div class=history markdown=1>
**History.** Prince **Władysław of Opole** brought the icon, probably from Ruthenia, and gave it to the Paulines whom he had brought from Hungary in **1382**. In **1430** Hussite raiders plundered the monastery and slashed the icon; when it was restored in Kraków, the painters could not make the new paint hold on the old surface, and the **two cuts on the cheek** were left, or painted in again. The panel is about **122 × 82 cm**; scholars date the present painting to the medieval period, in the Byzantine *Hodegetria* type ("she who shows the way"), Mary pointing to her Son.
</div>

<div class=legend markdown=1>
**Legend.** Tradition says **St Luke** painted the icon on a table-top made by Jesus from the house of the Holy Family, and that **St Helena** found it in Jerusalem and brought it to Constantinople. The dark colour has been explained by centuries of candle smoke, by the varnish, and in legend by a fire it miraculously survived.
</div>

### The siege of 1655 and the Queen of Poland
<div class=history markdown=1>
**History.** During the Swedish invasion of **1655**, the "Deluge", the small garrison of Jasna Góra under the prior **Fr Augustyn Kordecki** held out against a Swedish army for some forty days. Poles saw it as a miracle and the turning point of the war. In **1656**, at Lwów, King **John II Casimir** solemnly proclaimed the Mother of God **Queen of Poland**. In **1956**, under Communism, a million faithful renewed these vows at Jasna Góra, with the empty chair of the imprisoned Primate, Cardinal **Stefan Wyszyński**.
</div>

St **John Paul II** came here as a boy and young priest, and as Pope six times. "Totus tuus" ("wholly yours"), his motto, he entrusted to her; after the 1981 assassination attempt, he sent the bloodstained sash he was wearing to Jasna Góra. "His mother treasured up all these things in her heart" (Luke 2:51, ESV).
