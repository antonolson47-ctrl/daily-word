<div class=history markdown=1>
**History.** A Benedictine abbey founded in the 10th century on a granite island in a bay with some of the largest tides in Europe. It grew into the Gothic "Merveille" and later served as a prison. The Mont and its bay became a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 1979 ([UNESCO](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/80)).
</div>

<div class=legend markdown=1>
**Legend.** In 708 the Archangel appeared three times to Aubert, Bishop of Avranches, asking for a sanctuary on the rock. When Aubert hesitated, Michael pressed a finger into his skull. A skull with a hole, said to be Aubert's, is kept at the Basilica of Saint-Gervais in Avranches.
</div>

## Visiting (2026)
- **Abbey tickets:** €16 (1 Apr–30 Sep), €13 (1 Oct–31 Mar). Open 9:00–19:00 May–Aug, 9:30–18:00 Sep–Apr. Closed 1 Jan, 1 May and 25 Dec ([official info](https://www.abbaye-mont-saint-michel.fr/en/visit/practical-information)).
- Park in the paid mainland car parks and take the free **Le Passeur** shuttle, or walk about 45 minutes ([access & hours](https://montsaintmichel.gouv.fr/acceder-au-mont-saint-michel/horaires-et-tarifs)).
- **Never cross the bay sands without a licensed guide.** The tide comes in fast and there is quicksand.
