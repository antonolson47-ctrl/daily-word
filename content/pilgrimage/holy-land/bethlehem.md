"And she gave birth to her firstborn son and wrapped him in swaddling cloths and laid him in a manger, because there was no place for them in the inn" (Luke 2:7, ESV). Over the cave where Christians have honoured the birth of Christ since the 2nd century stands the **Basilica of the Nativity**, one of the oldest churches in the world in daily use.

## Good to know
- **Getting there:** Bethlehem is in the Palestinian West Bank, about 10 km south of Jerusalem. Go with a licensed guide or by taxi or bus through **Checkpoint 300**; carry your passport.
- **Hours (CIC, 15 September 2026):** the Basilica has **temporary hours of 5:00–17:00**. The **Grotto is closed for maintenance** except **Monday–Saturday 13:00–14:00** and **Sunday 11:00–15:00**. The Franciscans' morning Mass has moved to the Grotto of St Joseph. Check again before you go.
- **Next door:** the Catholic **Church of St Catherine** (Midnight Mass of Christmas is broadcast from here), **St Jerome's** cave beneath it, and the **Milk Grotto**.
- **Shepherds' Field** in Beit Sahour (Franciscan chapel, Monday–Saturday 8:00–17:00) and the Greek Orthodox Shepherds' monastery.
- **Support the Christians** of Bethlehem: buy olive-wood carvings and mother-of-pearl in Christian-run shops.

## Deep Dive

### The cave and the basilica
<div class=history markdown=1>
**History.** **St Justin Martyr**, born in Samaria, wrote around **150** that Jesus was born in a cave near Bethlehem, and **Origen** (about 248) says the cave was shown to visitors. Constantine's church was dedicated in **339**; the present basilica was rebuilt under **Justinian** in the 6th century. Tradition says the Persians spared it in **614** because the mosaic over the door showed the Magi in Persian dress. Its Crusader-era wall mosaics were restored in **2013–2019**. In **2012** the church and the pilgrimage route became Palestine's first **UNESCO World Heritage** site.
</div>

A **silver star** under an altar in the Grotto bears the words *Hic de Virgine Maria Jesus Christus natus est*, "Here Jesus Christ was born of the Virgin Mary". Its theft in **1847** was one of the sparks of the Crimean War and of the Status Quo of 1852. The **Door of Humility**, made low in the Ottoman period to stop carts and riders entering, makes every pilgrim bow.

<div class=legend markdown=1>
**Legends.** The white **Milk Grotto** is said to have turned white when a drop of the Virgin's milk fell as she nursed the Child during the flight into Egypt; women pray there for children. The tomb of the **Holy Innocents** is shown in the caves under St Catherine's, near the cell of **St Jerome**, who translated the Vulgate here.
</div>
