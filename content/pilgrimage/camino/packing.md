## Essentials
- Credencial, passport, cards, a little cash (small villages)
- Broken-in trail shoes or boots, and sandals for evenings
- 2–3 sets of quick-dry clothes, a warm layer, a rain jacket or poncho
- Sleeping bag or liner (public albergues give only disposable sheets)
- Headlamp, earplugs, eye mask
- Blister kit, sunscreen, hat, water bottle
- Small quick-dry towel, travel soap (doubles as laundry soap)
- Phone and charger. Offline maps or a Camino app such as [Wise Pilgrim](https://www.wisepilgrim.com/) or Gronze

## Tips
- Train with your loaded pack before you go.
- Anything you forget can be bought along the way, and Correos can mail extras home.
