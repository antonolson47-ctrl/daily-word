The only Camino that starts in Santiago. Pilgrims walk west to the Atlantic at Cape Fisterra, once thought the end of the known world, and to the Marian sanctuary of A Virxe da Barca at Muxía. Many walk a loop that takes in both.

<div class=warn markdown=1>⚠ Published distances vary (roughly 87–90 km to Fisterra, about 115–120 km if you add Muxía). Confirm on [Gronze](https://www.gronze.com/). Fisterra and Muxía issue their own certificates (the Fisterrana and the Muxiana).</div>
