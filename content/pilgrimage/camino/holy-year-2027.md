A Compostelan Holy Year (*Año Santo*, or Xacobeo) happens whenever **25 July**, the feast of St James, falls on a **Sunday**. The next one is **2027**. After that come 2032, 2038 and 2049.

| Date | What happens |
|---|---|
| 31 December 2026 | The Holy Door (*Puerta Santa*, on Praza da Quintana) is solemnly opened |
| All of 2027 | The door stays open; a plenary indulgence is offered under the usual conditions |
| 25 July 2027 (Sunday) | Feast of St James |
| 31 December 2027 | The Holy Door closes |

The motto of the Holy Year is from Matthew 2:10: *"they rejoiced exceedingly with great joy"* on seeing the star ([Archbishop's pastoral letter](https://catedraldesantiago.es/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Carta-Pastoral-AnoSanto2027.pdf)).

<div class=warn markdown=1>
⚠ **Confirm before you go**
- **Opening time:** reported moved to around **midday** on 31 Dec 2026, but the Cathedral's calendar still showed 5 pm when we checked.
- **Botafumeiro:** the giant censer is **out of service** while scaffolding for the cimborrio (lantern tower) restoration covers its mechanism, with no return date announced as of Oct 2026.
- **Pilgrim masses:** from 16 October 2026, the 9:30, 12:00 and 19:30 pilgrim masses move to the nearby church of San Agustín until further notice.

Check [catedraldesantiago.es](https://catedraldesantiago.es/) before your visit.
</div>
