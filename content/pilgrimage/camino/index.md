For more than a thousand years, pilgrims have walked across Europe to the tomb of the Apostle James the Greater in the Cathedral of Santiago de Compostela, in Galicia (northwest Spain). In 2025 a record **530,987** pilgrims received the Compostela certificate ([Pilgrim Office statistics](https://oficinadelperegrino.com/estadisticas-2/)).

## Quick answers
- **Shortest route that earns the Compostela:** Camino Inglés from Ferrol (~112 km, 5 days), or Francés from Sarria (~114 km).
- **Most popular:** Camino Francés (45.6% of 2025 pilgrims), then the two Portuguese routes (~36% combined).
- **Cheapest bed:** Galician public albergues, €10 a night, no reservations.

## The saint at the end of the road
The Camino ends at the tomb of the apostle James. Read his story, and what is history and what is legend, on our [St James the Greater (Santiago)](/saints/james-the-greater/) page. Saints along the routes include [St Fermín](/saints/fermin/) in Pamplona (Camino Francés), [St Eulalia](/saints/eulalia/) in Mérida (Vía de la Plata) and [St Elizabeth of Portugal](/saints/elizabeth-of-portugal/) in Coimbra (Camino Portugués), who walked to Santiago herself in 1325.

## Journey's end: the Cathedral of Santiago
Every Camino ends in the **Praza do Obradoiro**, before the cathedral that keeps the tomb of the Apostle James. The Romanesque cathedral was begun in **1075** and consecrated in **1211**. Pilgrims arriving here usually follow a series of traditional rites, which the cathedral describes on its [Rites of the pilgrim](https://catedraldesantiago.es/en/pilgrimage/) page.

### Pilgrim's Mass and the Compostela
- **Pilgrim's Mass:** daily at **9:30, 12:00 and 19:30**. While the high altar is being restored, it is **temporarily held in the Church of San Agustín**, a short walk from the cathedral. Mass in English is at **10:30** in the Pilgrim's Office chapel, daily except Wednesday. Backpacks are not allowed inside.
- **Botafumeiro:** used on major feasts and at some Masses. **The cathedral says it is temporarily not operating because of restoration work.** Check the [Masses and Botafumeiro page](https://catedraldesantiago.es/en/liturgy/) before you go.
- **Hours:** the basilica and the tomb are open daily from **7:00 to 21:00**; enter by the Praterías door. The museum, which includes the Portico of Glory and the roofs, is open 10:00–20:00 ([Visits](https://catedraldesantiago.es/en/visits/)).
- **The Compostela:** issued by the **Pilgrim's Office**, Rúa Carretas 33, open 9:00–19:00. You need at least the last **100 km on foot or horseback**, or **200 km by bike**, with your credential stamped (twice a day over the last 100 km). See [the Compostela](https://oficinadelperegrino.com/en/pilgrimage/the-compostela/) and our page on the [credential and Compostela](/pilgrimage/camino/credencial-compostela/).
- **Holy Year 2027:** the **Holy Door** on the Praza da Quintana opens on 31 December 2026. The jubilee indulgence is gained by visiting the tomb and praying, with Confession and Communion.

<div class=history markdown=1>
**Pilgrim's prayer** (from the cathedral): *"Saint James, Apostle, chosen among the first. You were the first to drink the Cup of the Master and you are the great protector of pilgrims; make us strong in faith and happy in hope on our pilgrim journey following the path of Christian life and sustain us so that we may finally reach the glory of God the Father. Amen."*
