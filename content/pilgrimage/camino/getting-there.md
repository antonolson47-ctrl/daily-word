| Route | Fly into | Then |
|---|---|---|
| Francés | Biarritz (BIQ), Bordeaux, or Madrid / Pamplona | Train via Bayonne to Saint-Jean-Pied-de-Port; or bus from Pamplona |
| Portugués | Porto (OPO), Lisbon (LIS) | Walk from the city, or train to Tui/Valença |
| Norte | Bilbao (BIO), San Sebastián, Biarritz | Train/bus to Irún |
| Primitivo | Asturias (OVD) | Bus to Oviedo |
| Inglés | A Coruña (LCG), Santiago (SCQ) | Bus/train to Ferrol |
| Vía de la Plata | Seville (SVQ) | — |

**Going home:** Santiago airport (SCQ) has flights to Madrid, Barcelona and several European cities. High-speed trains link Santiago with Madrid, and buses run to Porto.

<div class=warn markdown=1>⚠ Schedules and seasonal flights change. Check the current timetables from SNCF/Transports 64 (Saint-Jean), Renfe, ALSA and the airlines before booking.</div>
