- **May–June and September–October** are the most popular windows: mild weather, though they're also busy. In 2025, May and August were among the busiest months for arrivals in Santiago ([santiago.es statistics](https://santiago.es/en/camino-de-santiago/pilgrim-statistics)).
- **July–August:** hot on the Meseta and the Vía de la Plata, and the last 100 km are crowded with Spanish holidaymakers.
- **November–March:** quiet and beautiful, but many albergues close, days are short and rain is constant in Galicia. The Napoleon route over the Pyrenees is closed in winter; use Valcarlos.
- **2027 Holy Year:** expect record numbers all year, peaking around **25 July** (feast of St James).

<div class=warn markdown=1>⚠ Seasonal albergue closures change every year. Check [Gronze](https://www.gronze.com/) for the places on your route.</div>
