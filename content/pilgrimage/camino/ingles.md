Medieval pilgrims from England, Ireland and northern Europe sailed to Ferrol or A Coruña and walked from there. The shortest route that earns a full Compostela, and ideal for a single week.

<div class=warn markdown=1>⚠ Starting at **A Coruña** is only ~72 km, too short for the Compostela on its own. Start at Ferrol. (Pilgrims who started a route on foot outside Spain need at least 70 km in Spain; check the Pilgrim Office rules.)</div>

## Good to know
- **Fly into** A Coruña or Santiago, then bus/train to Ferrol.
