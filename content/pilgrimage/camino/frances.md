The most historic and most walked route, described in the 12th-century Codex Calixtinus. It crosses Navarra, La Rioja, Castilla y León and Galicia, through Pamplona, Burgos and León. Its biggest drawback is crowding, especially from Sarria onward.

## Good to know
- **Day 1 is the hardest:** about 1,200 m of climbing over the Pyrenees. In winter the Napoleon route is officially closed; take the Valcarlos road.
- **Short on time?** Sarria to Santiago (~114 km, 5 days) earns the Compostela. 67% of Francés pilgrims start there, so it's busy.
- **Getting to the start:** fly to Biarritz or Bordeaux → train to Bayonne → train/bus to Saint-Jean-Pied-de-Port; or Madrid → Pamplona → bus.
