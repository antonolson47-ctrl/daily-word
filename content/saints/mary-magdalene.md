## Her story
A woman from Magdala on the Sea of Galilee, from whom Jesus cast out seven demons (Luke 8:2). She followed him and supported his ministry, stood at the cross, and was the **first witness of the Resurrection**: Jesus sent her to tell the disciples (John 20:11–18).

## Path to sainthood
Venerated since the early Church (pre-Congregation). In **2016 Pope Francis** raised her memorial to a full **feast**, the same rank as the apostles, calling her the "Apostle to the Apostles".

## Patronages
Converts, penitents, perfumers, hairdressers, and contemplatives.

## Trivia
- In a sermon of 591, **Pope Gregory the Great** merged her with the unnamed "sinful woman" of Luke 7, creating the long-lived but unbiblical image of her as a repentant prostitute. The 1969 calendar reform separated them.
- A legend says she showed the Emperor Tiberius an egg that turned red, a story linked to the custom of red Easter eggs ⚠ (legend).
- French tradition places her last years in a cave at **La Sainte-Baume** in Provence.
