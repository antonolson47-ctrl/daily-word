## His story
Dominic de Guzmán was a Castilian priest, a canon of Osma, who while travelling through southern France met the Cathar movement and decided that the Church needed well-educated, poor, itinerant preachers to answer it. He gathered companions in Toulouse, and in 1216 **Pope Honorius III** approved the Order of Preachers (the Dominicans). He sent his friars to the great university cities, Paris and Bologna, making study central to the order. He died at Bologna in 1221.

## Path to sainthood
Canonised by **Pope Gregory IX** in **1234**, just 13 years after his death.

## Patronages
Astronomers, the Dominican Republic.

## Trivia
- A popular tradition says the Virgin Mary gave Dominic the **rosary**; historians see the rosary developing gradually, and the story spread mainly from the 15th century.
- Famous Dominicans include **Thomas Aquinas**, Catherine of Siena (a lay Dominican) and Fra Angelico.
- Dominicans still use the initials **O.P.** (*Ordo Praedicatorum*) after their names.
