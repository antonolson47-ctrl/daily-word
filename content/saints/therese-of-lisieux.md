## Her story
The youngest daughter of a devout family in Normandy, Thérèse entered the Carmel of Lisieux at just **15** and died of tuberculosis at **24**. Her autobiography, *Story of a Soul*, describing her "Little Way" of doing small things with great love, became one of the most-read spiritual books of the 20th century.

## Path to sainthood
Beatified on **29 April 1923** and canonised on **17 May 1925**, both by **Pope Pius XI**. **John Paul II** declared her a **Doctor of the Church in 1997**.

## Patronages
Missions (with St Francis Xavier, despite never leaving her convent), florists, France (secondary patron), and people with tuberculosis.

## Trivia
- At 14 she broke protocol during a papal audience and asked **Pope Leo XIII** in person for permission to enter Carmel early.
- Her parents, **Louis and Zélie Martin**, were canonised together in 2015, the first married couple canonised as a pair.
- She promised to "spend my heaven doing good on earth" and to let fall "a shower of roses". Roses are her symbol.
