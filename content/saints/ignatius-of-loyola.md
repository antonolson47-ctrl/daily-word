## His story
Íñigo López de Loyola was a Basque nobleman and soldier whose leg was shattered by a cannonball during the defence of **Pamplona** in 1521. Recovering at Loyola with only lives of Christ and the saints to read, he was converted. He spent months praying in a cave at Manresa, where he drafted the *Spiritual Exercises*, then studied in Spain and Paris, where he gathered companions including Francis Xavier. In 1540 **Pope Paul III** approved their Society of Jesus. Ignatius ran it from Rome until his death, as the Jesuits spread schools and missions across the world.

## Path to sainthood
Beatified in 1609 and canonised by **Pope Gregory XV** on **12 March 1622**, in the same ceremony as Francis Xavier, Teresa of Ávila, Philip Neri and Isidore the Farmer.

## Patronages
Soldiers, retreats, the Basque Country.

## Trivia
- The Jesuit motto *Ad maiorem Dei gloriam* ("for the greater glory of God") is often abbreviated **AMDG**.
- He laid down his sword before the Black Madonna at **Montserrat** in 1522, a scene inspired by the chivalric romances he loved.
- Pope Francis was the first Jesuit pope.
