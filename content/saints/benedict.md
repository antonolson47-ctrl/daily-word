## His story
Almost all we know of Benedict comes from Book II of the *Dialogues* of **Pope Gregory the Great**, written about 50 years after his death. Born at Nursia (Norcia) in Umbria, he left his studies in Rome to live as a hermit in a cave at Subiaco, attracted followers, founded small monasteries and around 529 established the abbey of **Monte Cassino**. There he wrote his **Rule**, a short, practical and moderate guide to community life summed up later in the phrase *ora et labora*, "pray and work". His twin sister, St Scholastica, led a community of women nearby.

## Path to sainthood
Pre-Congregation saint. **Pope Paul VI** proclaimed him **Patron of Europe** in 1964 with the apostolic letter *Pacis Nuntius*.

## Patronages
Europe, monks, farmers, against poison.

## Trivia
- Gregory tells how monks tried to **poison** his wine and the cup shattered when he blessed it, which is why the St Benedict medal is used against evil.
- Monte Cassino was destroyed and rebuilt several times, last after the Allied bombing of 1944.
- The Rule of St Benedict is still followed by thousands of Benedictine, Cistercian and Trappist monks and nuns.
