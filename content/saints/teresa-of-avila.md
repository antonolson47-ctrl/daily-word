## Her story
A lively Spanish noblewoman who entered the Carmelite convent in Ávila. After years of lukewarm prayer, a deep conversion in her late thirties led her to found the reformed **Discalced Carmelites** with St John of the Cross. Her books *The Interior Castle* and *The Way of Perfection* are classics of prayer.

## Path to sainthood
Beatified in **1614** by **Pope Paul V**. Canonised on **12 March 1622** by **Pope Gregory XV**, on the same day as Ignatius of Loyola, Francis Xavier, Isidore the Farmer and Philip Neri. In **1970** **Pope Paul VI** made her the **first woman Doctor of the Church**.

## Patronages
Spain, writers, people in religious life, and headache sufferers.

## Trivia
- She died on the night Catholic countries **switched to the Gregorian calendar**: the day after 4 October 1582 was 15 October, which is why her feast is the 15th.
- A much-loved story has her telling God after a mishap on the road, *"If this is how you treat your friends, no wonder you have so few!"* ⚠ It isn't in her own writings or in the first biography; the earliest known version is from 1912, so it is probably legend.
- Bernini's sculpture *The Ecstasy of St Teresa* in Rome depicts her vision of an angel piercing her heart.
