## His story
Francesco Forgione, from a farming family in Pietrelcina near Benevento, joined the Capuchins and was ordained in 1910. From 1916 he lived at the friary of San Giovanni Rotondo. On **20 September 1918** he reported receiving the **stigmata**, wounds like Christ's, which he bore for 50 years. Vast crowds came for his Masses and his confessions, and he was investigated and at times restricted by Church authorities. He founded the **Casa Sollievo della Sofferenza** ("Home for the Relief of Suffering"), a hospital opened in 1956.

## Path to sainthood
Beatified on 2 May 1999 and canonised by **Pope John Paul II** on **16 June 2002**.

## Patronages
Civil defence volunteers, adolescents (popularly).

## Trivia
- As a young priest, **Karol Wojtyła** (the future John Paul II) went to confession to him in 1947.
- Many people reported extraordinary gifts such as bilocation and reading hearts; these are testimonies, not matters of faith.
- His body has been on display at San Giovanni Rotondo since 2008, one of Italy's most visited shrines.
