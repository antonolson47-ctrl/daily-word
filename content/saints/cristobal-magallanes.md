## His story
Cristóbal Magallanes Jara was born in **Totatiche**, Jalisco, and worked the fields as a boy before entering the seminary. As parish priest of his home town he built schools, a seminary, farm cooperatives and dams, and preached among the Indigenous Huichol people.

When the government closed churches in 1926, he kept his seminary going in secret. He **opposed armed rebellion**, writing against it, but he was arrested on 21 May 1927 on his way to celebrate Mass at a ranch. Four days later, on **25 May 1927**, he was shot without trial at **Colotlán**, along with a young priest, St Agustín Caloca. Before he died he gave away his remaining possessions to his executioners and said: "**I die innocent, and ask God that my blood may serve the union of my Mexican brothers.**"

The martyrdoms are documented by witnesses and records gathered for the cause. His final words are reported by several witnesses.

## Path to sainthood
Beatified in 1992 and canonised by **Pope John Paul II** on **21 May 2000** with **24 companions**, 22 priests and 3 laymen killed between 1915 and 1937.

## Patronages
Mexican priests (popularly).

## Where to visit
Totatiche (Jalisco): his parish and shrine; the Santuario de los Mártires, Guadalajara

## Trivia
- The 25 martyrs come mostly from the states of Jalisco, Zacatecas and Guanajuato.
- He founded a seminary in Totatiche that trained many priests even during the persecution.
- Several of his companions, like him, refused to take up arms but were killed simply for being priests.
