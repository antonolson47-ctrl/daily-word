## The story
Maria Goretti was born in Corinaldo in the Marche in 1890 to a poor farming family who moved to the Pontine marshes south of Rome, near **Nettuno**. Her father died of malaria when she was nine, and she looked after the house and her siblings while her mother worked the fields.

## The full story

### 5 July 1902
The family shared a house with the Serenelli family. **Alessandro Serenelli**, 20, had repeatedly tried to seduce her. On 5 July 1902, when she was alone, he tried to rape her. She resisted, saying it was a sin and he would go to hell. He stabbed her 14 times. She died the next day in hospital, after forgiving him: "I want him to be with me in Paradise."

### Alessandro's conversion
Serenelli was sentenced to 30 years. Unrepentant at first, he said Maria appeared to him in a dream in prison, giving him lilies, and he changed. After his release in 1929 he asked her mother's forgiveness; she forgave him, and they went to Mass together that Christmas. He was present in **St Peter's Square** at her canonisation in 1950, with her mother, and spent the rest of his life as a lay brother with the Capuchins.

<div class=history markdown=1>**History.** Her story is fully documented, including the trial and Serenelli's own testimony. The prison dream is his own account.</div>

## Path to sainthood
Beatified 1947 and canonised 24 June 1950 by Pius XII, before the largest crowd ever at a canonisation at that time; her mother, Assunta, was present, the first mother to see her child canonised.

## Patronages
Purity, young people, rape victims, forgiveness.

## Trivia
- Her body lies in the Sanctuary of Nostra Signora delle Grazie e Santa Maria Goretti in Nettuno.
- Her relics toured the United States in 2015 as the "Pilgrimage of Mercy".
