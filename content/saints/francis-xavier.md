## His story
A Navarrese nobleman studying in Paris, Francis Xavier was won over by his roommate's friend Ignatius of Loyola and became one of the first Jesuits. In 1541 he sailed from Lisbon for **Goa**, then worked in southern India, Malacca and the Moluccas, and in 1549 reached **Japan**, where he founded the first Christian communities. He died of fever in 1552 on the island of **Shangchuan**, off the coast of China, waiting for a ship to take him to the mainland.

## Path to sainthood
Beatified in 1619 and canonised by **Pope Gregory XV** on **12 March 1622**, together with Ignatius. Pope Pius XI named him patron of missions in 1927 (with Thérèse of Lisieux).

## Patronages
Missions, missionaries, India, Japan, Navarre.

## Trivia
- His body lies in the **Basilica of Bom Jesus** in Goa; his right arm, which baptised thousands, is kept in the Gesù church in Rome.
- His letters to Europe, describing Asia, were widely printed and inspired many missionaries.
- A legend says a crab returned his lost crucifix from the sea on the way to the Moluccas.
