## His story
Early sources record a martyr named Valentine buried on the Via Flaminia north of Rome; another tradition speaks of a bishop Valentine of Terni, and the two may be the same person. Beyond that, very little is known. Later legends say he healed a jailer's daughter and signed a farewell note "from your Valentine", or secretly married couples against the emperor's orders; these stories have no early source.

## Path to sainthood
Pre-Congregation saint. Because so little is known, his feast was **removed from the General Roman Calendar in 1969**, but he remains in the Roman Martyrology and is still honoured locally.

## Patronages
Lovers, engaged couples, beekeepers, epilepsy.

## Trivia
- The link between his day and **romantic love** first appears in late-medieval English poetry, notably Chaucer's *Parlement of Foules* (14th century).
- Relics said to be his are claimed by several churches, including in Rome, Terni and Dublin's Whitefriar Street church.
- On 14 February the General Calendar today celebrates **Sts Cyril and Methodius**, apostles to the Slavs.
