## His story
A brilliant London lawyer and humanist, friend of Erasmus and author of *Utopia* (1516), Thomas More rose to become **Lord Chancellor** of England under Henry VIII in 1529. He resigned in 1532 as the king broke with Rome over his marriage, and refused to swear the oath recognising Henry's supremacy over the Church in England. Imprisoned in the Tower of London, he was convicted of treason and beheaded on **6 July 1535**. Tradition records his last words as declaring he died "the King's good servant, but God's first".

## Path to sainthood
Beatified in 1886 and canonised by **Pope Pius XI** on **19 May 1935**, together with Bishop John Fisher. **Pope John Paul II** proclaimed him **patron of statesmen and politicians** in 2000.

## Patronages
Statesmen, politicians, lawyers, adopted children.

## Trivia
- His life inspired Robert Bolt's play and film ***A Man for All Seasons***.
- He wore a **hair shirt** under his chancellor's robes; one survives.
- His head was displayed on London Bridge; his daughter Margaret Roper is said to have rescued it, and tradition says it lies in a family vault in Canterbury.
