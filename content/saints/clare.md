## Her story
A young noblewoman of Assisi, Clare was moved by Francis's preaching. On the night of Palm Sunday 1212 she slipped away from home, and at the Portiuncula Francis cut her hair and gave her a rough habit. She settled at **San Damiano**, where she led a community of women in strict poverty for over 40 years, often ill but never giving up what she called the "privilege of poverty". Her sisters, the **Poor Clares**, spread across Europe.

## Path to sainthood
Canonised by **Pope Alexander IV** in **1255**, only two years after her death.

## Patronages
Television, eye disease, embroiderers.

## Trivia
- Two days before she died, **Pope Innocent IV** approved her Rule, the first rule known to have been written by a woman.
- **Pope Pius XII** named her patron of **television** in 1958, because when she was too ill to attend Mass she reportedly saw and heard it on the wall of her room.
- Tradition says she turned back Saracen soldiers attacking San Damiano by holding up the Blessed Sacrament.
